##  Avatar: The Last Airbender Overview
*Avatar: The Last Airbender* is an animated fantasy series about a boy named **Aang**, the last Airbender, who must master all four elements to restore balance to the world.

## Main Characters
- Aang
- Katara
- Sokka
- Toph
- Zuko

## Why I Love It
1. Amazing character development
2. Great world-building
3. A perfect mix of humor and emotion

> "Hope is something you give yourself. That is the meaning of inner strength." — Uncle Iroh

## Coco Overview
*Coco(2017)* is an acclaimed animated fantasy-adventure film produced by **Pixar Animation Studios** and released by Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures. Directed by Lee Unkrich and co-directed by Adrian Molina, the movie celebrates the Mexican holiday Día de los Muertos (Day of the Dead).

## Main Characters
- Miguel
- Mama Coco
- Hector
- Ernesto
- Abuelita

## Why I Love It
1. Very Musical
2. Many Exciting parts
3. Very engaging and thrilling

> "Remember me" — Hector
